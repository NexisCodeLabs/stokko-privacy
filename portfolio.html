About

From industrial automation to Android development.

I hold a Master's degree in Automation & Industrial Computing. During my final year, I built an IoT solar-energy monitoring system at Algeria's Renewable Energy Development Center (CDER) — acquiring sensor data and building a real-time Android interface to visualize it. That project convinced me that the most interesting problems live at the intersection of hardware and software.

Since 2021, I've worked as a freelance Android developer, designing and shipping custom applications for clients across different platforms — including apps published on Google Play. NexisCodeLabs grew out of that work: a small studio built around tools that actually hold up in daily, real-world use.
